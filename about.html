Built on one block

Why UniStyle only makes a handful of styles — and why they all share the same pattern.

How we started

A wardrobe, not a catalog

UniStyle started with a simple frustration: too many racks split into "men's" and "women's" sections selling nearly identical clothes cut two different ways. We asked a small pattern-maker to draft one clean block — a tee, a jacket, a trouser — sized from XS to XL, and built the label around just that.

Every piece we release has to earn its place next to the others. If it doesn't hold up after a hundred washes, it doesn't go on the site.

What we stand for

Three things we won't compromise on

01

One size range, no gate

Every style is graded across the same XS–XL range. You shop by fit and preference, not by a label that assumes who you are.

02

Small batches, real seams

We cut and sew in short runs with a partner workshop, which means better quality control and far less unsold stock.

03

Honest pricing

The price on the tag is what you pay — no checkout surprises, and no payment details required until your order is on its way.
